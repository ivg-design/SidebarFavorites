# Round 2 delight

Context (inferred from codebase, high confidence): audience = Mac users who care how Finder looks; brand = precise,
dry, craft-oriented (no mascots, no confetti). Delight strategy: "subtle sophistication" — the page behaves like Finder.

## Already delightful (keep)
- Rail rows that turn from grey folders into glyphs as you scroll — the page demonstrates the product on itself.
- Hero focal glyph is a real vector morph (Rive) from the folder into the chosen symbol.
- Split-flap numerals; the coral trace line drawing itself; Copy → check with a "Copied" pop.

## Added this round
1. Before/After: a Finder-style hint pill inside the panel ("Click a row to change its icon") and a chevron that appears on
   row hover — the first click is now obvious and the popover pops from the row.
2. Custom icons: the live Preview toy sits directly under the real Preview screenshot, so dragging the slider is visibly
   "the same panel, alive".
3. Both icons: the real dialog, cropped to the exact warning and three radios the demo mirrors.
4. Finder mocks: every glyph from one SF-style pen, so the hero sidebar reads as one real window.
5. Rive handoff: the fallback silhouette stays until the canvas has fully faded in — no dip in the focal glyph.

## Deliberately not added
- No easter eggs, sounds, confetti or playful copy: wrong register for this product.
- No parallax on the vivid panels: the gradients are wallpapers, not motion.
